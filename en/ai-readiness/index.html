Why standard approaches don't work
The consulting firm approach

Interviews and frameworks

A generic AI maturity model applied through stakeholder interviews. The output is a slide deck with capability gaps and a transformation roadmap. It tells you what you already know and offers no evidence from your actual data.

The system integrator approach

Sell first, discover later

The vendor assesses your readiness as a precursor to selling their platform. The recommendation is inevitably their product. Data quality issues are minimised or deferred to the implementation phase.

What we do instead

Evidence from your actual data

We access and analyse the operational data your business already generates — with the same experimental rigour applied to physics research. Null hypothesis, signal-to-noise quantification, uncertainty bounds. The assessment is evidence-based, not framework-based. If the data does not support the investment thesis, we say so before budget is committed.

Why it matters

Measuring badly is worse than not measuring

An AI model trained on misaligned, inconsistent, or low-signal data will produce outputs that look plausible and are wrong. The cost of discovering this after deployment is an order of magnitude higher than the cost of this assessment.

Four deliverables in four weeks
01

Data Landscape Map

A structured inventory of every data source in the process scope: PLC logs, MES records, quality system exports, maintenance records. For each source: schema, granularity, actual availability, and a first-pass quality assessment. Not what IT believes you have — what you actually have when someone reads the files.

Format → structured report + source schema table
02

Signal-to-Noise Assessment

Statistical analysis of which variables in your existing data carry predictive information about quality, efficiency, or scrap — and which are redundant or noise. Typically 60–70% of variables in a multi-system environment are eliminated at this stage. What remains is the usable signal surface for any subsequent AI work.

Format → variable ranking with correlation evidence + uncertainty bounds
03

AI Use Case Prioritisation

The 2–3 use cases with measurable ROI achievable within 12 months, ranked by technical feasibility and economic impact. Each use case includes: data requirements already met vs. missing, estimated model performance range, investment estimate, and expected payback window. Designed to support a capital allocation decision.

Format → decision matrix with ROI range per use case
04

Build / Buy / Wait Recommendation

For each prioritised use case, an explicit recommendation: build internally, procure a vendor solution, or defer until data quality or technology maturity improves. Includes vendor landscape for the relevant category and the specific technical criteria any solution must satisfy. This is the document you bring to a board conversation.

Format → structured recommendation with go/no-go rationale
How the assessment runs
WK 01 · ACCESS + INVENTORY

Get to the raw data

On-site or remote access to operational data sources. No IT project required — we work with whatever is available: exports, read-only database access, existing integrations. We inventory what exists and assess its quality before forming any hypothesis.

WK 02–03 · ANALYSIS

Measure signal quality

Statistical analysis of variable distributions, cross-system alignment, temporal consistency, and predictive correlation with quality output. Gaps and inconsistencies are documented, not glossed over. This is where most assessments find the unexpected.

WK 04 · SYNTHESIS

Produce the deliverable

Use case prioritisation, build/buy/wait recommendation, and a final readout session with your technical and business leadership. The deliverable is designed to be read by a CFO and scrutinised by a process engineer.

A management consultant maps your AI maturity against a framework and produces a transformation roadmap. A system integrator assesses your readiness as a precursor to selling their platform. Neither produces an independent, evidence-based answer to the question that actually matters: does the AI investment thesis hold up against your real operational data?

The method comes from experimental physics: you do not assume the hypothesis is true before testing it. You design the measurement, run it on real data, and report what you find — including when the finding is that the data does not yet support the investment case. That answer, delivered before budget is committed, is worth more than a confidence-building exercise dressed as a readiness report.
